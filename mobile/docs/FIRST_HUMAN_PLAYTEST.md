# First human playtest

Try a short, uncoached session. Stop whenever you want. There is no minimum
session length and no required experiment. Read the technical report afterward.

Life opens the visual catalog. Food adds a finite handful. Mutagen places one
temporary exposure area. Flow stirs the water. Tap life to follow, drag to pan,
and pinch to zoom. Wide shows the tank. Pause / Play controls time; the adjacent
speed button cycles 1×, 2× and 4×. ↺ starts a new world after confirmation.

The observer should not rank specimens, predict mutations, explain strategies,
or tell the player what is supposed to be exciting.

Afterward, ask:

1. What did you want to try, and could you do it comfortably?
2. What held your attention? Did any organism or descendant matter to you?
3. Could you form a hypothesis about what happened?
4. Would you try another experiment? What would you change?

Record the player's account before explaining mechanisms. Separate confusion,
control friction, boredom, attachment and curiosity. Automated checks cannot
certify enjoyment. Current evidence and limitations: [build handoff](playtest-pass/HANDOFF.md).
