# Accepted pivot: strategy persistence

The owner accepted a narrower causal investigation after the retrospective.
Do not introduce further resource forms, roles, or speculative physiology while
this comparison is unresolved. Chemistry is archived outside the playable engine.

## Frozen reference

The productive spatial model: 32×32 torus, four finite 64-site beds separated by
14 units, radii 1.8/2.7/3.6/4.5, capacity .4 per site, incident supply 8/s with
linear light falloff over 30 units, no current. Eight developing founders, one
single constructor and one five-cell sensor/motor/depot genome per bed, placed
on real resource sites. All ordinary energy costs and physics retained; 360-cell
capacity. No source movement during the primary comparison.

## Matched regimes

Each seed starts from exactly the same bodies, positions, controllers and energy.
Only inherited initial mutation probabilities differ, equally for both founder
branches. No condition reacts to population outcomes.

- fixed: all mutation probabilities zero; injury can still change physical bodies.
- small: existing neural (.28) and geometry (.18) mutations, existing small
  magnitudes; all other categories including meta-mutation zero.
- full: unchanged M14 inherited mutation regime, including structural and meta.

Start with seeds 1–20, one simulated hour in every regime. Extend predeclared seeds
1–4 to five hours in every regime. Extinction and technical-capacity encounters
remain in the dataset. Freeze the executable and archive source for each batch.

## Observer

Track every created organism by ID and ancestry outside the engine. Record mature
births, juvenile losses, lifespan, inherited cell/organ counts, observed energy
uptake, actual paid thrust, and starvation exposure. Initial ancestry is an
observation label and never enters biological rules. Distinguish inherited body
capacity from cells lost to damage. Observed uptake omits any absorption during
a cell's final removal tick; global energy conservation is verified separately.

Sample population and regional state every 60 simulated seconds. Use complete
lifecycle records, rather than endpoint survivors alone, to assess reproduction.
The observer must not consume simulation RNG or alter world state; verify it
against an unobserved run from the identical initial world.

## Decision

If unchanged foragers repeatedly lose to simple organisms, diagnose the physical
resource/competition economics. If they persist but small/full mutation loses
function, examine viable neighboring mutations and the costs of inherited change.
A difference in one seed is insufficient. Use timing, reproduction and energy
traces to identify the failure mechanism before making the next systemic change.
